I scanned UK government repository commits to estimate what percentage of commits were made from government vs
non-government email addresses.

I found 22075 UK government repositories based on the list of organisations on the [_Who's using
GitHub?_](https://government.github.com/community/) page.

Only ~2500 (~11.3%) repositories had data in commits.ecosyste.ms. This is the data I used, so hopefully the subset is
representative.

Because many of the email domains in the dataset were not included in
[_world-gov-domain-names_](https://github.com/SoftwareHeritage/world-gov-domain-names), I classified domains ending in
`gov.uk` as government domains, and I also added in a few more manually identified domains.

- 235,661 (17.9%) commits came from government domains
- 1,080,145 (82.1%) commits came from non-government domains
